Why PBKDF2 + Unique Salt?

A Rainbow Table is a precomputed lookup database mapping millions of plaintext passwords to their SHA-256 hashes. Without a salt, an attacker who leaks your database can instantly crack any password with a table lookup.

❌ Unsafe: SHA-256 Alone H = SHA256("password123")
  • Rainbow table instantly cracks in <1ms
  • GPU: ~100 billion guesses/sec
  • Two users with same password → same hash
✅ Safe: PBKDF2 + Salt H = PBKDF2(pwd, salt, 100000)
  • Rainbow tables useless (unique salt per user)
  • ~10,000 guesses/sec (100,000× slower)
  • Identical passwords → completely different hashes
